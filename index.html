About us

More Than Cleaning.A Story of Vision, Hard Work & Opportunity.

RC Tally Cleaning Service, LLC was built on a simple belief: when hard work, integrity, and opportunity come together, something meaningful can be created.

Like many entrepreneurial stories, ours was not built overnight. It was built one opportunity at a time, one client at a time, and one project at a time. Through hard work, consistency, and a willingness to take on new challenges, RC Tally has continued to grow from providing cleaning services into handling larger commercial, property, and project-based opportunities.

At RC Tally, we understand that clients are trusting us with their businesses, properties, and projects. That is why our goal goes beyond simply completing a job. We want to build long-term relationships and become a company our clients know they can depend on.

Our story is still being written.

What started as the vision of an immigrant entrepreneur has become a growing local business with an even bigger vision for the future: to create jobs, develop people, expand our services, and build a company recognized throughout Tallahassee and beyond for quality, reliability, and integrity.

About the founder

Our founder, Jhonny Rodríguez, began his professional journey far from Tallahassee. Born and raised in the Dominican Republic, Jhonny pursued a career in dentistry, becoming a dentist and developing a professional foundation centered on precision, discipline, attention to detail, and caring for people.

But Jhonny also had an entrepreneurial spirit.

When he immigrated to the United States, he brought more than his professional background with him. He brought a vision—the determination to build something of his own, create opportunities for his family and others, and establish a business founded on trust and excellent service.

That vision became RC Tally Cleaning Service.

The principles Jhonny developed throughout his professional career remain at the heart of the company today: attention to detail, responsibility, integrity, professionalism, and a commitment to doing the job right.

Services

What we clean

Homes, offices, and clinical spaces. Pick the one that fits, or call and we’ll put together a plan for your space.

Recurring Home Cleaning

Weekly, biweekly, or monthly upkeep on a set schedule. Kitchens, baths, floors, dusting, trash — the whole rotation.

Deep Cleaning

The reset. Baseboards, vents, grout, inside the oven and fridge, behind and under the things that normally get skipped.

Move-In / Move-Out

Empty-unit cleans for tenants, landlords, and realtors. Cabinets, closets, and appliances cleaned inside and out.

Office & Commercial

After-hours cleaning for offices, salons, clinics, and retail. Restocking and trash-out included on every visit.

Post-Construction

Fine dust removal, sticker and adhesive cleanup, window and fixture polish so the space is ready to hand over.

Windows & Pressure Washing

Interior and exterior glass, plus driveways, walkways, and siding washed down to the original color.
