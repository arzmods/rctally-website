About the founder

Meet Our Founder

Our founder, Jhonny Rodríguez, began his professional journey far from Tallahassee. Born and raised in the Dominican Republic, Jhonny pursued a career in dentistry, becoming a dentist and developing a professional foundation centered on precision, discipline, attention to detail, and caring for people.

But Jhonny also had an entrepreneurial spirit.

When he immigrated to the United States, he brought more than his professional background with him. He brought a vision—the determination to build something of his own, create opportunities for his family and others, and establish a business founded on trust and excellent service.

That vision became RC Tally Cleaning Service.

The principles Jhonny developed throughout his professional career remain at the heart of the company today: attention to detail, responsibility, integrity, professionalism, and a commitment to doing the job right.
